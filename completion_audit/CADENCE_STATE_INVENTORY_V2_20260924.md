## Proof boundary

The tested namespace-copy/phase mechanisms preserve **the original loop's supplied-time decisions and captured mutable data**, including actual fair inference. They do not prove real process scheduling, input acquisition, interprocess transport, all external-service state, or a connected lifecycle for newly emitted micro entries. Those omissions are acceptance blockers, not states implicitly assumed equivalent. The complete requested equivalence determination therefore remains **FAIL / NOT DEPLOYABLE**.
