## Separation and contribution

Historical and already inspected live samples are development evidence. Baseline certification uses a new fixed, untouched forward period; its end must not depend on current accuracy. Recommended registration is 14 calendar days, reporting the complete period even if signals are rare. Fewer than 200 qualified FINAL calls or fewer than 500 official contracts is explicitly insufficient for a strong 93–95% claim; these are reporting evidence floors, not signal gates, and passing them alone never certifies the goal.

Do not tune on that holdout. If development evidence supports a candidate, freeze its thresholds/weights before a new future holdout. Compare incumbent and candidate on identical contracts with the same data/latency/publication gates. Report paired wins/losses, coverage and timing changes for all lanes. Confidence intervals should account for serial dependence, for example by resampling whole UTC days. A gain in one metric cannot conceal damage to another.

For each ladder report pairwise overlap, unique covered contracts, same/opposite-side agreement, timing advantage and conditional outcomes. These measurements show complementary outputs, not causal model contribution. Any feature/weight ablation must replay only past-available inputs with preserved chronological state, and must undergo the same separated evaluation. No redundancy removal or numeric tuning is presently justified.

Report all outages, censoring and incomplete paths. Do not call the present aggregate scoreboard certified.
