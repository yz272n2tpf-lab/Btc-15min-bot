## Required product contract, before any lifecycle implementation is promoted

**Owner boundaries.** EARLY is the primary value-entry money ladder. Core SCALP/reversal is the intracontract money ladder. FINAL is the confirmation and protection-evidence layer for EARLY, with its existing earlier/cheaper opportunity retained when supported. V8.1 keeps its separate native owner. Every entry/advice record must name exactly one ladder owner and policy version. No shared global “position” inferred from whichever card updated most recently.

**Immutable origin.** An advisory entry must record `entry_id`, owner, contract, side, native decision ID/time, publication time, original quoted ask, quote proof identity/epoch, target/open/close, causal BRTI/BTC source and receipt clocks, model identity and entry policy version. Once born, these fields cannot be rewritten by new probability, quote, FINAL confirmation, restart or display refresh. A manually reported fill, if supported later, is a separate user fact with its own time/price; a signal must never claim the user executed it.

**Deduplication and repeats.** Repeated delivery of the same entry ID is idempotent. A durable ledger is required before restart can create duplicate advice births. Existing native/shadow repeat gates remain in their own domains. EARLY re-entry eligibility, Core re-entry eligibility, same-side repeats, opposite-side arbitration, cross-ladder conflicts and restart treatment are **UNDEFINED POLICY — REQUIRES LATER EVIDENCE-BASED VALIDATION**. Do not transfer the true-scalp 120-second gate to EARLY. Until a policy is approved, no newly implemented ledger may invent a replacement entry or silently suppress an original native output.

**Advice authority.** Only a specified native-clock owner may change advice state; informational frames are never transition events. Any future use of faster evidence as an advice trigger needs separate explicit lifecycle approval. Each advice record references the unchanged `entry_id`, original origin, current native evaluation ID, causal source proofs, reason and policy version. It is advice only, not an order or a claim of execution.

| Advice state | Required meaning and linkage | Missing policy |
|---|---|---|
| HOLD | Fresh, qualified evidence and an approved rule support retaining advice for this exact entry. | Qualification and transition rule need validation; HOLD must not be a default on failure. |
| CAUTION | An approved deterioration condition is met for the same entry, with source/time and evidence visible. | Numeric/temporal conditions and recovery arbitration remain undefined. |
| PROTECT | Approved protection advice for the same immutable entry; any arm state and peak/target/trail state belong to that owner and its native clock. | Which shadow rule, if any, becomes advice authority is not approved. No threshold is copied into live authority. |
| EXIT | An approved native-clock exit condition produces terminal exit advice with current qualified executable-side bid/time and reason. Original entry is retained. | Stop/target/trail priority, horizon and execution acknowledgement policy need validation. EXIT advice does not mean a manual position was closed. |
| FLIP | Explicit linkage between exit advice for one origin and a separately qualified opposite-side entry with a new ID. | Opposite-entry qualification, timing, repeat and cross-ladder arbitration need validation. An opposite probability preference alone is not FLIP. |
| WAIT / UNAVAILABLE | Current advice cannot be qualified; suppress current source-dependent guidance while retaining historical origin and last authoritative event as history. | No invented HOLD, exit, replacement entry or “safe position” claim. |

**FINAL advising an existing EARLY origin.** Join by EARLY `entry_id` and contract, not by latest preferred side. FINAL confirmation adds a separate evidence record with its own timestamp, probability and source proofs. It never changes the EARLY entry time/price, turns a late expensive quote into the original entry, or creates a second entry implicitly. Agreeing/disagreeing and weakening FINAL evidence may be displayed descriptively now. Mapping that evidence into HOLD/CAUTION/PROTECT/EXIT/FLIP requires a validated advice policy. Earlier/cheaper FINAL capability remains possible under the frozen entry conditions; no new probability, timing or price thresholds are chosen here.

**Rollover.** The old contract's ledger remains attached to its original contract and origin. Mark contract closure/expired advice separately from a user execution or an inferred profitable exit. Use the existing qualified official closeout evidence for settlement facts when available; otherwise retain unresolved status. A new contract requires a new native-qualified origin. Never relabel an old entry/proof to the new contract.

**Failure and recovery.** Stale/future BRTI, interrupted quotes, owner change, missing provenance, uncertain contract or incomplete features produce current WAIT, with original entry history retained. Recovery must requalify current sources and preserve original origin; query count cannot arm protection or create persistence. Restart requires explicit owner generation and durable idempotency before any new connected lifecycle may be called complete. UI transport expiry is independent of the action clock and must redact stale current advice, not refresh it from an information frame.

**Release boundary.** This specification is the minimum structural contract. Undefined policy remains visibly undefined and requires later evidence-based validation using permitted development data. It is not permission to tune or deploy a policy, and does not use the permanently validation-only September cohort. The finished connected signal-only product remains incomplete until these lifecycle connections and policies are separately validated and approved.
