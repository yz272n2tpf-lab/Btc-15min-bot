1. Diagnosis of the prior forecast failure

The prior ridge models predicted BID at 240/300/360/420 seconds. They trained
on 97/95/92/87 labeled independent origins and calibrated on 56/51/48/45.
The empirical fifth-percentile residual penalties were -42.74/-62.03/-57.51/
-58.66 cents. Consequently, even sizable point forecasts usually could not
cover current ASK, fees and reserve. The opened 16-contract October4 window
had only 4/4/2/2 usable first-origin endpoint labels; MAE was 18.88/24.82/
41.60/43.24 cents. Lower-bound coverage on a handful of labels does not
establish useful uncertainty calibration.

It screened 505/469/433/376 observations yet admitted zero entries. At those
same fixed endpoints it missed 3/2/1/1 positive first-origin quote scenarios;
these overlap across horizons and cannot be added as independent trades.
Current BID and spread encoded entry ASK implicitly; model confidence and
BTC/BRTI gaps were used. Entry price and direction were not wholly omitted.
However, one 60-second momentum value could not represent persistence,
multiscale reversals, volatility/structure, evolving settlement evidence or
condition-triggered exit timing. Training used one origin per contract but
screening covered many later observations, adding distribution-shift risk.

The calibration penalty was an unconditional tail residual, not a validated
conditional bound on the mean for the particular setup. It was economically
very conservative, but removing it would not solve the large point errors or
establish safety. Exact endpoint regression targets a different question from
whether a directional opportunity remains justified as conditions evolve.
These are documented objective/model limitations; this study does not pretend
to statistically isolate the causal contribution of each omitted feature.

2. Implemented approach and scope

New files are confined to candidates/early_condition_20261011 and
audit/early_condition_20261011. engine.py is a pure in-memory reducer with no
network, database, order, worker or deployment integration. native.py validates
original native inputs using the existing validators. study.py fits/replays
condition-exit economics; acceptance.py evaluates the frozen rules; retained
normalized observations permit wholly offline reproduction.

The prototype first identifies a structural directional setup. It then needs
an independently supported empirical distribution of gains at the times its
OWN exit conditions fire. It does not forecast an exact future BID, substitute
fair value for liquidation value, search future maxima, or score settlement
correctness as resale profit. All outputs retain actionable=false,
production_enabled=false, signal_only=true, orders=false, manual_fill=null and
realized_profit=null. No trusted production profile has been installed.

  both short momenta are adverse, and BID deteriorates. Boundaries start at
  signed30s lows at entry and can advance only on a new same-side BID high
