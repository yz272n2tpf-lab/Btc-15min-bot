Data acceptance
1. Register a consecutive contract universe before opening its outcomes. Include
   no-call contracts, outages, both directions and complete decision streams.
   Split by contract/day, never random rows. Preserve a separate untouched final
   evaluation set. Purge overlaps from fitting/calibration/horizon validation.
2. Bind each exact native frame to deployment, build, model artifact/weights,
   official contract/open/close/target, decision and publication timestamps,
   source-known-at receipts, quote epoch/sid/sequence, BTC/BRTI source times,
   causal features and fee metadata. Hash the original frame and data manifest.
   Repeated polls are not independent observations. Reject conflicting duplicates.
3. Preserve every original BRTI second and its receipt/revision history. At time
   t use only receipts known at t. Do not backfill gaps with later CF revisions.
   Samples must belong to [close-60,close), one value per second as then known.
   Missing past observations are unknown, not extra future time. Respect the
   exact official contract rounding/tie rules in model labels and evaluation.
4. Join official finalized result/settlement receipts only as outcome labels,
   never as predictors. Reject target/window conflicts and document exclusions.
   Minute OHLC and selected-call tables cannot reconstruct executable quote age,
   depth, publication authority or the original partial-settlement path.

EARLY forecast/profile validation (currently missing)
5. Pre-register the opportunity estimator, uncertainty method and horizon family
   using development data. Its output must concern the SAME-SIDE liquidation BID
   after an evidence-supported directional horizon, not the existing model fair.
   Train/evaluate directly against timestamped quote paths, with fees and a
   measured manual-execution reserve/latency budget. Ask-to-bid includes spread
   crossing; additional reserve must not be represented as another actual fill.
6. The family must represent larger directional opportunities, not short scalp
   moves. The human-reviewed profile must exclude short-horizon scalp profiles.
   Do not invent a universal contract-time cutoff or train on the 5-cent incident
   alone. Four to seven minutes are examples, not a mandatory duration grid.
7. Validate profitable-exit potential, loss tails, missing/depleted depth,
   calibrated uncertainty, regime support, holding-path deterioration and
   calibration drift. Estimates of a mean are not guarantees for an individual
   trade. Verify coverage at legitimate early/middle/later portions of contracts.
8. Pin the reviewed model/profile, validation report, horizon evidence and manual
   execution evidence hashes. Complete validation before evaluation contracts
   open. Supply exact frame-bound prediction receipts. A caller's 'validated'
   boolean alone must not establish trust. The candidate registry is EMPTY by
   default; only synthetic fixtures currently have explicit test trust.
9. Test warnings on confidence-only noise, independent market deterioration,
   partial-average challenges and plan expiry. Compare warning lead time,
   warning/exit churn, premature exits and missed deterioration. Do not optimize
   a new probability-drop threshold on the evaluation set. The proposed WATCH
   corroboration/recovery rule has unit validation, not observed trading-quality
   validation. Existing EXIT rules also require performance assessment; no
   fixture pass establishes profitability or manual execution.
10. Exercise multi-entry contract sequences with fresh evidence, quote continuity,
    immutable prior BUY/EXIT history, invalidation then recovery and reversal.
    Re-entry does not imply the user closed the prior manual position. Preserve
    one recommendation owner and explicit original signal identity.

12. Study a settlement-aware supplement and replacement separately. Require a
    prevalidated causal remaining-path distribution conditioned on observed

    Keep the original >=90% model gate; any proposed settlement-model threshold
    needs a separate preregistered approval, not an optimized after-the-fact rule.
    The current evaluator deliberately marks these alternatives unscorable
    without validated settlement evidence.
13. Primary unit: earliest qualified lock per contract per variant. Report UP
    and DOWN separately. Report contradictory/reversed later locks separately,
    without counting repeated polls as independent successes. Compare new
    contract coverage AND earlier timing on commonly locked contracts; a variant
    may change the side of the first call, so score each own first-call side.
14. Report n, correct/incorrect, false-lock rate, accuracy uncertainty, Brier/log
    loss, reliability by preregistered confidence bands, calibration slope,

    all exclusion counts; do not select convenient examples.
15. Wilson intervals are only descriptive independent-contract intervals. Use
    paired day-block uncertainty for comparisons because decisions/contracts
    share market conditions. Pre-register minimum effective sample sizes and

    set; the current task supplies no validated margins, so none are invented.
16. Acceptance requires no unacceptable false-lock increase, calibrated new-call
    cohorts, useful added/earlier calls and stable performance across regimes,
    not merely a higher headline accuracy or more signals. If evidence remains
    insufficient, retain current 75/50 and 90% gates.
