WATCH/HOLD. The candidate adds a narrow warning rule: uncorroborated probability

support returns to its pre-warning probability anchor with a later quote and
otherwise supportive evidence. No fixed probability-drop threshold was invented.
This is a proposed risk policy implemented and unit-tested offline; its observed
warning quality, missed deterioration and churn performance remain unvalidated.

The candidate also warns when a causal partial settlement hurdle challenges the
held side even if latest BRTI and spot remain favorable, when the original
opportunity horizon ends, or when its manual-execution budget no longer fits.
Missing settlement evidence is visible. Those additions generate WATCH, not an
automatic timer/average-based EXIT. A brief probability fluctuation alone does
not EXIT. Existing stronger protection remains latched rather than repeatedly

directional/economic invalidation with valid source/model/fee evidence. Missing
