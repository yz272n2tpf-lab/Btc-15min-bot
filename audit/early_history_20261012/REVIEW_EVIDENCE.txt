2. MAIN SOURCE-REVIEW FINDING: PRE-OPEN UNDERLYING HISTORY
The accepted architecture explicitly permits genuine pre-open BTC/BRTI underlying
history while keeping contract quotes, targets, tickets and origins separate.
The delivered continuation_engine uses self.history from the base engine; that
base history is cleared on rollover. continuation.causal_vector requires every
feature row to share the current contract, target, close and epoch. The study also
creates a new engine for each contract.
No separate underlying-history input is present in the delivered continuation classes.

This is a source-inspection finding: the planned pre-open-history route has not been
implemented in this delivery. It is additional to the documented missing historical
evidence. It does NOT prove that adding that route would yield profitable predictions.
