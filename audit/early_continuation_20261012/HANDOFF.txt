  (5) min(signed BTC120 move, signed BRTI120 move)/BTC120 range;

  at most 15 s early. Older half includes midpoint; newer excludes it. No interpolation.

  A negative 120 s feature is allowed; there is no universal positive-long-trend veto.
  MACD, RSI, settlement probability and future-best BID are not predictors.
  solve/fit/calibrate: training-standardized pooled logistic, L2 C=1, unpenalized
  intercept, whole-contract total weight 1 across both sides and labeled landmarks.
  Calibration is a single intercept only. No feature/threshold/model search.
  ContinuationEstimator: pinned artifact, exact method/code identity, shape/numeric
  checks, chronology, evidence domain, context and marginal feature support checks.
