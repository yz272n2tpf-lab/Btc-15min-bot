1. RECOMMENDATION AND SCOPE

RECOMMENDATION: REDESIGN THE ENTRY OBJECTIVE AND SELECTION LAYER; REUSE THE REBUILT MECHANICS. Do not activate the current formulation, lower its safeguards, or discard the project solely because the current exploratory estimator is unfavorable.

Verified code behavior: the latest candidate recognizes a joint 30-second BTC/BRTI impulse, short-path efficiency and limited quote deterioration, then requires 10 seconds of confirmation. Ordinary 25-55c entries do not require a favorable 120-second trend, persistent movement over that longer window, or a forecast of a durable resale opportunity. The current estimator answers a different, narrower question: expected quote return conditional on a delayed entry and a completed path ending under the unchanged baseline EXIT manager.

Observed evidence: the detector sometimes recognizes a move before it becomes expensive, sometimes recognizes a failing counter-move, and sometimes precedes a sustained favorable quote path. The inherited 147 complete scenarios average -12.4231c after assumed costs; 18 are positive. The latest estimator made zero selections under its frozen positive-economics diagnostic rule. Neither these results nor the code establishes that EARLY exclusively selects bursts, that every longer trend filter helps, or that FINAL/EXIT caused the losses.

Proposed hypothesis: use the existing independent UP/DOWN setup engine as an onset detector, and evaluate one small model whose target is a durable, delayed-entry resale opportunity. Add only longer price-path context and developing structure to the existing four causal features. Keep thesis monitoring and FINAL protection separately measured. Do not promote the new objective until an independent, bounded comparison supports it.

5. CURRENT ESTIMATOR AND EXECUTION SUPPORT

S5 resale.py freezes one ridge=1 fit with four standardized features: ASK cents, runway minutes, minimum signed BTC/BRTI30 move divided by BTC30 range, and minimum BTC/BRTI30 efficiency. Separate direction/decision-context cells require at least12 complete training contracts. Contract weights sum to one within each cell. Training used all 122 Sep26-27 contracts; the 174 later contracts are exposed diagnostics, not an untouched holdout.

The label is100*(first admissible observed selling BID after baseline condition EXIT plus 10s - refreshed delayed-entry ASK - both fee stresses - reserve). Entry uses the first observed row after 10s, within a further10s tolerance; cancellation during the wait stays canceled. Exit uses the first admissible row in the 10-20s window after EXIT availability, not a future maximum. Path identities, continuity and immutable origin bind to retained records. All 147 complete inherited labels matched in the prior pass. This audit did not regenerate them.

Comparison costs remain one-contract, whole-cent-ceiling0.07*p*(1-p) each leg plus max(2c, qualification spread, entry spread, exit spread). These are labeled stress assumptions, not verified account fees. Payout capacity, settlement correctness and model fair-minus-ASK are excluded from this estimator's target.

Loading checks pinned artifact digest, schema, METHOD, finite shapes, baseline management source hash and training-label availability. Engine rejects a nondefault Rules object or alternate manager class when this estimator is attached. Supported inference must occur after the last training label and inside every training feature min/max. This prevents silent extrapolation; a rectangular min/max envelope is still not proof of dense joint support.

10. BUY, THESIS MONITORING, EXIT AND FINAL

The latest rebuild already separates mechanical ownership correctly: recognition creates a setup; qualification creates a ticket; only explicit manual confirmation or explicit simulation creates an origin. An unfilled canceled ticket owns no management state. Existing management updates do not suppress new setup calculations. Manual occupancy prevents conflicting new entries; that is an exposure policy, not an EXIT-prediction gate.

Statistical coupling remains: the resale label uses the baseline EXIT manager, and loader/engine identity checks require that policy. This coupling is honest for estimating that exact policy's net quote outcome. It is inadequate as the sole measure of whether an attractive entry can develop into a durable resale window. Earlier opportunities may close before EXIT; paths with no EXIT have no numerical label; different management identities invalidate the artifact. The fit conditions on both surviving delayed entry and path completion, so canceled and censored paths do not become forecasted outcomes.

Baseline management is S3 CoreEngine.manage. Weakness flags are short BTC/BRTI momentum loss, lost favorable target gap, adverse120s context when available, BID30 deterioration by at least max(spread,1c), and a verified causal last 60s settlement contradiction. MODEL_OPPOSITION is reported but excluded from warning count. At least two weakness flags persisting10s cause WATCH; <=25s execution room also warns.

EXIT requires a corroborated breakdown persisting15s and at least two observations: both target gaps and both short moves adverse; or both 120s/30s sources adverse plus BID deterioration; or causal settlement contradiction plus momentum/BID deterioration; or both signed prices breaking ratcheted origin structure by0.10*range plus adverse short moves/BID. No profit prerequisite and no4-7-minute hold timer. Structure ratchets only with a new observed best BID and both short moves favorable.

Baseline warning recovery requires 15s without warning, short momentum loss, lost favorable gap or longer reversal. This is coherent conservatism for the former favorable-gap entries, but a state incompatibility for a pre-crossing origin whose approach recovers before the target is crossed. The latest optional ThesisRecoveryManagement corrects that specific internal state: baseline exits run first; pre-crossing only; renewed recognition, positive short moves, positive120s when available, quote support, execution room, no structure/settlement/data contradiction and no FINAL latch;15s sustained recovery. It is not enabled by default and cannot attach the baseline-trained estimator without rejecting the policy identity.

Inherited matched management comparison:209 origins; all reconstructed baseline terminals matched; candidate terminals also identical on all 209 paths. Internal states changed on25 paths (13UP/12DOWN),165 observations. Combined hypothetical outcomes did not improve. This is evidence for a state correction, not evidence that FINAL or early EXIT caused losses. Full native FINAL state is absent on these paths, so native FINAL-protected lifecycle performance remains unverified. [S11]

In the native product route, FINAL's90% confidence, <=480s remaining, BTC gap >=$75 above 360s remaining/otherwise$50, distance/range>=1, target-side agreement and BRTI agreement with absolute gap>$11 remain separate. Qualified opposition or loss after established same-side strong FINAL latches protection. The latest wrapper reuses these native rules; it does not recreate or change them. Native EARLY management additionally uses model weakening/value and one-/five-minute momentum, a different manager from the research baseline. [S8]

11. ONE PROPOSED CANDIDATE: CAUSAL CONTINUATION OPPORTUNITY

This is one design for review, not implemented code or a validated strategy. Preserve the latest onset screen and all common price, reaction, identity and execution boundaries. Replace its economic selection objective with the probability that an attractive, delayed-valid entry develops a durable net-positive observed resale window before material adverse movement or the contract deadline. Retain the old resale estimate as a separately labeled baseline-policy diagnostic, never relabel it as this probability.

Recognition: continue independent UP/DOWN30s onset detection and 10s confirmation. Do not require target crossing or settlement-model agreement. Compute120s BTC/BRTI context from genuinely available source history, including legitimate pre-open underlying-price history when available. Underlying history can cross a contract boundary; old Kalshi quotes, targets, tickets and origins cannot. Missing120s data yields context-unavailable, not synthetic history. This avoids imposing a new two-minute wait at every official rollover when real pre-open data already exists.

Use exactly six model inputs, direction-normalized: (1) current ASK cents; (2) remaining minutes after receipt delay; (3) current joint30s impulse/range; (4) current joint30s efficiency; (5) minimum signed BTC/BRTI120s movement divided by BTC120s range; (6) developing structure, the minimum across BTC/BRTI of the change in signed adverse extrema between the older and newer nonoverlapping60s halves, divided by BTC120s range. Signed adverse extrema are lows for UP and negative highs for DOWN. Use the same range floor and causal endpoint rules; do not interpolate missing observations.

These two added context features ask whether progress extends beyond the latest impulse and whether pullback structure is improving. They do not prove persistence. In an established trend, longer progress and advancing structure should support the prediction; in a developing reversal, adverse older direction can coexist with improving recent structure. Do NOT impose a universal both-positive120s veto: cases C/D show why its benefit is unproved. Mixed sources, low efficiency and alternating/nonadvancing structure should reduce support or cause abstention. An inexpensive contract or fresh burst alone is insufficient.

One explainable method: pooled direction-normalized logistic regression, L2 penalty C=1, training-standardized features, no interactions, no indicator tournament. Whole-contract weights sum to one across all landmarks and sides. Use one frozen scalar intercept calibration on the chronological calibration contracts; no slope/threshold tuning. This is a proposed method, not evidence of its adequacy. Preserve direction and target-location strata in reporting even though the fit is pooled.

Scoring and freshness: use at most one landmark per10s per developing setup while common eligibility holds. Train on that same landmark rule; all landmarks from a contract remain in one chronological partition. On evaluation, take the first qualifying landmark per genuine setup; cancel/recheck using fresh evidence after the declared10s manual delay. Refreshed tickets require fresh model inference at the declared cadence, a current support check and the original short source lease. Never extend the lease with an old score. Earlier unsupported landmarks remain in the ledger; a later supported one is explicitly another dependent observation, not an independent opportunity.

Price attractiveness: retain25-55c primary and no normal authorization above 58c. Sub25 and 56-58c remain WATCH until separately supported; the first candidate trains/evaluates primary prices only. Score while price is attractive, rather than waiting for a completed multi-minute winner. When delay moves ASK outside the band, record a canceled/too-expensive opportunity. Do not chase or erase it. Scoring may still be too late; that is a required measured failure mode.

For the proposed research selector only, freeze predicted durable-opportunity probability >=0.75 and nominal block-bootstrap lower bound >0.50, plus support and common execution eligibility. These are proposed preregistration criteria, not a reduction of FINAL/legacy confidence or evidence of calibrated75% accuracy. They may yield zero selections; do not lower them to generate alerts. Validation and economic readiness remain separate from a numeric forecast. No threshold in existing code is changed.

Keep MACD/RSI display-only in this first design. Do not add a separate MACD crossover, RSI band, five-minute sign veto, trend score and acceleration gate on top. Their incremental information is unestablished. The proposed additions use price history already conceptually required, not another collection-dependent indicator stack.

Thesis monitoring retains immutable origin and existing deterioration/EXIT protections. Store the six decision features and their later causal evolution as explanations; strengthening/weakening does not itself create an alternative EXIT. Use the optional pre-crossing recovery component only as a separately identified state comparison. Keep default baseline EXIT for the first matched combined-outcome audit. No best-later-price optimization or fixed holding clock.

Re-entry retains genuine30s adverse reset, fresh onset/confirmation, new supported forecast, current affordable price and explicit closure of a manual origin. Same-run refreshes or a momentarily cheap quote do not become new setups. Future evidence may show this reset misses resumption after a mild pullback; that would require a separately justified change, not another branch in this candidate.

12. PROPOSED OUTCOME: DURABILITY WITHOUT A PERFECT EXIT

The current condition-EXIT net target should remain a baseline-policy metric. The new BUY target must be defined independently of that manager. The following operational definition is proposed for review and must be frozen before any fitting. Its10c/60s/-10c numbers are explicit product/evaluation choices, NOT learned optima, promised profit or live stop instructions. They are deliberately a materially larger opportunity than a momentary small quote uptick; changing them after results would constitute a new study.

At a causal landmark, wait the declared10s entry reaction. Use the first admissible fresh observed ASK/depth state within the existing additional10s tolerance. Revalidate common recognition, confirmation, identity, supported quantity, price and known cost inputs. Label construction must not depend on a future fitted-model score or future management warning. Any additional model-score cancellation by the eventual ticket policy is recorded separately in the delivery scorecard; it cannot rewrite the underlying path label. Any intervening qualification loss remains canceled, as in the retained comparison. A cancellation counts as failure to deliver an entry opportunity for this prediction target, not a trading loss.

For each subsequent admissible same-side selling BID, compute net liquidation cents per intended contract using that entry ASK, applicable entry/sale fees for the declared size and reserve=max(2c, decision/entry/current-exit spreads). Retain the old fee-stress version separately if applicability is unknown. Fees/quantity cannot be silently imputed. Future quote depth is evaluated at its own receipt time, never forecast from entry depth.

Define a durable opportunity certificate as an OBSERVED supported-price episode with net liquidation >=+10c, spanning at least60s and at least three advancing quote observations, with no stale, broken-sequence or insufficient-size interval counted as support. At the existing6s quote freshness limit, a genuinely supported60s span necessarily needs many more than three observations or documented continuing valid book state. Missing intervals break the certificate; do not fill them by interpolation. Record the actual span, quote count, cadence and fraction of elapsed time covered.

After the certificate becomes observable, assess the FIRST admissible selling BID after another 10s exit reaction, with 10s tolerance. Require that delayed quote still supports size and >=+10c net. This is a causal liquidation benchmark for evaluating manual opportunity, not an instruction to sell at the best point. It does not presume the certificate's first or highest BID was executable. A brief profitable tick that vanishes before the certificate/reaction cannot count as a durable success.

Competing event: a valid net liquidation observation <=-10c before durable success marks an adverse-first failure for the primary opportunity label. This is an evaluation barrier, not a new stop-loss rule. Keep any later rebound in descriptive records, but it does not rewrite that outcome as a safe success. Observe until the contract's last admissible exit-reaction point, not a fixed holding duration. No success before the deadline is a failure only if observation was complete; source loss or unknown fee/depth becomes censored/unassessable.

Primary labelY=1 only for a delayed-valid, durable, delayed-liquidation-supported opportunity before the adverse event/deadline. Known entry cancellations and fully observed failures areY=0 for opportunity delivery. Censored cases retain unknownY and their reason; do not fit them as negatives or pretend complete-case calibration describes all attempts. Publish worst/best-case success bounds over all selected attempts, with missing cases counted as failures/successes respectively. If missingness makes the conclusion depend on that choice, stop as inconclusive.

Report secondary path measurements without turning them into alternate selection targets: time to first net-positive and+10c BID, adverse movement before success, durations of favorable observed intervals, covered time, BTC/BRTI30 agreement occupancy,120s structure trajectory, price-window loss and actual observed reaction latency. Distinguish: burst-only (favorable quotes but no durable certificate on a complete path); durable opportunity; adverse-first/immediate failure; no meaningful favorable excursion/choppy or sideways evidence; and unavailable. A sideways price outcome alone does not prove market chop; confirm the causal path descriptors separately.

Do not use settlement result, maximum future BID, fair-minus-ASK or a fixed4-7-minute sale. The baseline-manager delayed net remains a separately reported combined BUY+EXIT outcome. A candidate could improve durable-opportunity identification while still losing under that manager. Such a result would be an objective-specific research finding, not a profitable complete strategy or grounds to rewrite FINAL.
