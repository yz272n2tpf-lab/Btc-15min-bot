3. A separate current-model limitation

The fitted current calibration is p(flip)=sigmoid(2.9910579826854993*q - 2.278633903090592), where RF probability q lies in [0,1]. Therefore p(flip) lies in [9.2908%,67.0937%]. Reaching 75% would require q=1.1291, which is impossible. Maximum stay probability is 90.7092%.

Consequently the current model cannot satisfy EARLY's 75% requirement for a side opposite its current BTC target side, whether UP from below the target or DOWN from above. It can qualify a cheap same-target-side quote when one exists. The archived winners all agreed with their noncausal target-side feature; they do not demonstrate genuine pre-crossing prediction skill.

This is a mathematical capability limit, not proof that the true reversal probability should exceed 67.1%. Changing calibration to force stronger confidence would manufacture support unless independently validated. No calibration, confidence floor, FINAL model or production threshold was changed.
