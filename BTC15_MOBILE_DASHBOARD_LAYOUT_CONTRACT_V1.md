# BTC15 Mobile Dashboard Layout Contract V1

**PRESENTATION ONLY | SIGNAL ONLY | MANUAL EXECUTION | NO ORDERS**

This document freezes the dashboard view-model/layout behavior before any live dashboard integration.

## Card order

1. FINAL OUTCOME
2. EARLY OPPORTUNITY
3. CONTRACT TIME LEFT
4. SCALP OPPORTUNITY
5. FLIP RISK

The order is stable on both devices. iPhone stacks the cards in one column. iPad may use two columns, but the semantic order remains the same.

## One timer

Only `CONTRACT TIME LEFT` renders a visible countdown. FINAL, EARLY and SCALP may reference the canonical timer as a data source, but must not render independent countdowns.

## Fixed content slots

### FINAL OUTCOME
Fixed supporting rows:
- Confidence
- Kalshi Price
- Entry Quality
- Reason

Reason is capped to two visual lines. Longer explanations truncate/expand in a detail view rather than changing the main card height.

### EARLY OPPORTUNITY
Fixed supporting rows:
- Kalshi Entry
- Model Fair
- Model Edge
- Entry Quality

### SCALP OPPORTUNITY
Fixed supporting rows:
- Entry Price
- Current Sell Price
- Profit Now
- Best Profit
- Profit Given Back
- Profit Protection

Missing values render as an em dash. Rows never disappear solely because a value is temporarily unavailable.

## Entry-language authority

The frozen App Language Contract remains authoritative:
- 25–35c: IDEAL ENTRY
- 36–50c: GOOD ENTRY
- above 50c: TRACKING ONLY / DON'T CHASE
- below 25c: LOW PRICE / CHECK SIGNAL

Price guidance never changes the backend detector state.

If a scalp was internally ACTIVE/PROTECT/EXIT but its original entry was not an acceptable manual-entry path, the user-facing card remains TRACKING ONLY / NO POSITION ASSUMED. Internal lifecycle state may remain available in diagnostics only.

## Protection / exit

User-facing `PROTECT PROFITS` / `EXIT NOW` may appear only when the original scalp entry was an acceptable manual-entry path under the frozen presentation bands.

The existing +5c arm / 4c giveback lifecycle remains unchanged. The prospective 1c/2c/3c Watch-warning research is not exposed until separately certified.

## State-driven visual tone

Card tone may change only when a semantic state changes, for example:
- WAIT -> ACTIVE
- ACTIVE -> PROTECT
- PROTECT -> EXIT
- entry tier GOOD -> DON'T CHASE
- FINAL WATCH -> FINAL LOCK

Raw price ticks by themselves must not cause a white/yellow or other blink when the semantic state/tier has not changed.

## Transition stability

Within one contract/opportunity:
- opportunity index cannot go backward;
- completed scalp count cannot decrease;
- PROTECT cannot regress to ACTIVE;
- EXIT cannot regress to PROTECT/ACTIVE;
- stale source may fail closed to WAIT;
- a later scalp opportunity may restart at ACTIVE;
- contract rollover may reset the lifecycle.

## Flip Risk

Until separate numeric calibration/certification is complete:
- card title: FLIP RISK
- value: NOT CALIBRATED (or a separately validated categorical label later)
- no numeric percentage

## Safety

- no order button or order action generated by the view-model;
- manual execution only;
- no blended EARLY/FINAL/SCALP accuracy;
- no threshold tuning in presentation code;
- no production deployment from the research branch.
